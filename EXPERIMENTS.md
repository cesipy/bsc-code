### 1.3 What the finished runs say so far

1. **Pretrain-seed variance is not measured, and the early repeats suggest it is large.** The two early pretrains (seeds 1570, 1571) differ by 0.0105 HM AUROC, 0.0057 UPMC acc and 0.0354 MM-IMDB F1 **[parsed]**, i.e. 2.6–15× the within-pretrain finetune SD of DF1. Late seed 1570 (HM 0.7716) sits +0.008 above the thesis late mean (±0.0041). Each pretrain has a single finetune seed equal to its pretrain seed, so pretrain and finetune variance are confounded. n = 2 — a warning, not an estimate. The thesis ± covers finetune seeds only, so every fusion-depth gap in the 0.005–0.02 range is currently unsupported. **[inference]**
2. **Delayed fusion (HM / UPMC):** DF1 0.7274 < DF3 0.7415 < DF2 0.7455 < thesis late 0.7637 on HM, and 0.9065 < 0.9225 < 0.9261 on UPMC. MM-IMDB macro-F1 is flat (0.518–0.521). The "clear progression" in the journal holds only for HM and UPMC, on one pretrain per config with different pretrain seeds. DF3's log header says `pt+ft_delayed_fusion_2` while its vision ids are [7,8,9] (name mismatch). **[parsed]**
3. **Reverse direction (REV, text late / vision early)** is about as bad as the other early-vision variants (HM 0.7183), so *vision* being fused early looks costly, but this is also single-seed. **[inference]**
4. **Alignment jumps sit at each stream's first cross-attention index** (late at logged layer 9, early at 3, DF1 at 3 with no extra step at 5). Epoch-0 HM mkNN / CKA: late L9 0.19 / 0.40; early L3 0.11 / 0.19; DF1 L3 0.18 / 0.34; REV L3 0.28 / 0.70. REV is the strongest early alignment and drops at L9, where text fusion starts. **[parsed]**
5. **The printed `delta` is `cycle_kNN(T,V) − cycle_kNN(V,T)` with k = 32 (`src/analysis.py:413-419`) computed on N = 1024 samples** (all 1032 printed values lie on a 1/1024 grid; `bs-analysis: 128` is only the forward batch). At epoch 0, 82 of 96 layer values are negative and layers 0–2, before any cross-attention, are already negative in 20/24 values. So the asymmetry most likely comes from the unimodal encoders or from the geometry of the two spaces, not from fusion **[inference]**. Single-layer `delta` magnitudes are indistinguishable from sampling noise: median cross-task difference at identical weights is 0.020 vs median |delta| 0.025; only the sign pattern is a candidate signal. **[parsed]** The audit adds that the sign *meaning* is unsettled (§2.0, M1): `cycle_knn(A,B)` hops through **B's** neighbours first (`src/metrics.py:50-52`, **[verified in code]**), while the docstring names the LLM first, and the journal's "positive = text space more reliable" has no support in the code.
6. **Metric noise floor** at identical weights (switching the task changes the alignment loader): median |Δ| mkNN 0.010, CKA 0.030, cycle-delta 0.020; SVCCA varies up to 0.05 across seeds on identical inputs (`torch.svd_lowrank` is randomised). **[parsed]**
7. **HM test AUROC exceeds val AUROC by +0.0195 on average** (SD 0.0099, n = 10); MM-IMDB and UPMC gaps are ≈ 0. Selection uses val AUC, so HM test numbers carry a small positive offset vs val. **[parsed]** (late seed 1570: val 0.7576 vs test 0.7716 **[verified]**.)
8. UPMC selects epoch 15 (end of budget) for DF1 seeds 1568/1569, so those finetunes may be under-trained. **[parsed]**

### 1.5 Data-quality caveats

- The ± convention in the journal is population SD (ddof = 0), inferred from DF1 only.
- `procr` (logged, e.g. 22.59) is `metrics_llmrepsim.orthogonal_procrustes` on L2-normalised, **uncentered** features: `sqrt(‖X‖²+‖Y‖²−2·nuc)`, a *distance* (lower = closer) with maximum √(2N) ≈ 45 at N = 1024 (`src/metrics_llmrepsim.py:67-77`, `src/analysis.py:452-454` **[verified in code]**). It grows with depth (≈ 3 at layer 0 → ≈ 22 at layer 11 in the late-1570 log) while CKA also grows, so it cannot be read as "higher = more aligned" and the README's similarity wording is wrong. Do not mix it into rankings until it is replaced (§2.0, M5).
- Pre-10-17 logs print `cos_sim`, which the current code no longer prints — the analysis code changed between eras.
- 2026 pretrain AP accuracies (0.88–0.93) are well above the journal's 27.06 early value (0.786); the logs do not explain why. The memory note about the 2026-05-31…06-07 mkNN-loss window may account for the older low values **[inference]**.
- `log_2026-10-17gpu6.txt` lines 1–108 re-collect F1 for 12 older DF1/DF2 finetunes and print each block twice; that is the only F1 source for those runs.

## 2. Proposed ideas (ranked)

### What I would do next (ordered; nothing here has been started)

1. **Pin the measurement conventions (no compute, ≈ 1 h).** (a) Settle the cycle-kNN hop order and sign for `delta` against the Wittgenstein paper (arXiv 2605.09352, definitions not accessible to the audit) and write the direction explicitly into the metric names; (b) decide the discovery split. `program.md` wants a *validation* cohort with an immutable paired-ID manifest, but the alignment helper uses the Hateful Memes **test** split, first N unshuffled (`src/datasets/get_datasets.py:174`).
2. **Write the session contract and build the evaluator (CPU, tests first).** A checkpoint-analysis driver that loads a pretrain checkpoint, extracts paired CLS features once and caches them, then computes the metrics and permutation nulls on the cache (design in `metric_code_audit.md` §11, prototype in `audit_scripts/null_proto.py`). Fix M1–M5 on the way. No training, no test-set evaluation.
3. **Pilot on existing checkpoints (one GPU, ≤ 1 h, ≤ 6 trials, per program.md §3):** Hateful Memes, 2025 baseline / early / late pretrains, N = 1024, ≈ 200 permutations; report raw and calibrated CKA, mkNN, cycle-kNN in **both** directions, SVCCA (exact), Procrustes similarity, and effective rank per layer. Then the same on the 2026 early ×2 and late seed-1570 pretrains to see how much the *measurements* move between pretrains (P1a).
4. **Delayed-fusion checkpoints (DF1–3, REV) on a 12×12 text-layer × vision-layer grid** with nulls, because the fusion schedule pairs layers ordinally (below) while the current analysis only compares layer *i* with layer *i*.
5. **Only then decide on GPU-heavy work:** finish late-1570 finetunes (P4, ≈ 10 h), more finetune seeds for DF2/DF3/REV (P5), and whether a reduced-recipe pretrain-variance study (P1b) is worth its own contract.

**Fusion schedule, verified by reading `src/vilbert.py:180-250` and by the audit running it on mock layers:** cross-attention block *k* is applied after text layers `0..t_k−1` and vision blocks `0..v_k−1` have run, using `zip(v_ids, t_ids)` ordinal pairing, and it updates both streams. For `--t-ids 3 4 5 --v-ids 5 6 7` the three blocks see (text after layer 2, vision after block 4), (3, 5), (4, 6): the vision stream has two extra unimodal blocks before it first interacts, while the text stream interacts earlier. `--v-ids 3 4 5 --t-ids 9 10 11` (REV) is the reverse. `ViLBERTConfig`'s defaults (t = 6..11, v = 0..5) make the first block read raw patch embeddings. `t_ids`/`v_ids` are not validated for order or equal length (`zip` silently truncates).
